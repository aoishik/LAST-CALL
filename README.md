# LAST CALL
Last Call CLI Based Game
Credits: Special thanks to (@Arsukie in GitHub)[https://github.com/arsukie] and also Hack Club YSWS jame gam for inspiration and support.

## Description
LAST CALL is a CLI interface based game with the theme "Trust No One". The game is set in a research facility called Blackwood Research Facility. You have 13 minutes to investigate and 10 calls to collect evidence. But everyone will tell a differenr story, the info may or may not be correct. Decide who to trust or trust no one and make your final decision before time runs out.

## Gameplay
You are given 30mins and 10 calls termed as battery. You can investigate different places to find evidence. You can also call the employees there to here their side of the story but remember they may or may not be corrct. Each investigations take 90 secs of your time and each call takes 1 battery and 30 secs of your time. The info you find may condratict with other characters. You can accuse someone if you have enough evidences, accuse no one, or leave the facility, with your choices determining the ending of the game.

## How to Play
1. Arrow keys to navigate the menu.
2. Enter to select an option.
